About This Course

The internet powers the modern world, but many people don't understand what happens behind the screen. This course introduces students to the technology that makes websites, apps, and online services possible.

Students will explore how computers communicate, how websites are delivered, and how different technologies work together to create the digital experiences we use every day.

What You Will Learn

  • How the internet works and how devices communicate
  • Understanding networks, servers, and clients
  • How websites are hosted and delivered to users
  • The difference between browsers, websites, and web servers
  • Introduction to IP addresses and domain names
  • How data travels across the internet
  • Understanding cloud computing basics
  • Introduction to APIs and how applications communicate
  • Basics of cybersecurity and protecting online systems
  • Modern internet technologies shaping the future

Course Details

Level: Beginner to Intermediate

Duration: 6 weeks

Format: Online one-on-one or group lessons

Ideal For: Students interested in technology, coding, networking, and how the digital world works
