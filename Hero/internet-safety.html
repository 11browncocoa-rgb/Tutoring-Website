About This Course

The internet provides incredible opportunities for learning, communication, and creativity, but it also comes with risks. This course teaches students how to use technology safely and responsibly.

Students will learn how to recognize online threats, protect their personal information, and develop good digital habits.

What You Will Learn

  • Understanding online privacy and personal information
  • Creating strong passwords and securing accounts
  • Recognizing scams, phishing attempts, and suspicious messages
  • Safe browsing habits and responsible internet use
  • Understanding social media safety
  • Protecting devices from common online threats
  • Managing your digital footprint
  • Learning basic cybersecurity concepts
  • How to respond to unsafe situations online

Course Details

Level: Beginner

Duration: 4 weeks

Format: Online one-on-one or group lessons

Ideal For: Students, parents, beginners, and anyone wanting safer online habits
