About This Course

Digital skills are essential in today's world. This beginner-friendly course helps students understand the fundamentals of computers, online communication, productivity tools, and safe internet usage.

Whether you are preparing for school, work, or everyday life, this course gives you the confidence to use technology effectively.

What You Will Learn

  • Understanding computer hardware and software
  • Using files, folders, and computer settings
  • Internet browsing and online research skills
  • Email communication and digital professionalism
  • Creating documents and presentations
  • Introduction to spreadsheets and data organization
  • Online safety, privacy, and cybersecurity basics
  • Using modern digital tools for productivity

Course Details

Level: Beginner

Duration: 6 weeks

Format: Online one-on-one or group lessons

Ideal For: Students, beginners, and anyone wanting to improve their technology skills
